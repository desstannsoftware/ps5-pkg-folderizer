### Fixed

- Games on PS5 extended storage and USB drives show up now. The console hides `/mnt/ext0`, `/mnt/ext1` and `/mnt/usb*` from the app, so 01.024.000 could not see them; the helper now makes every connected drive visible to the app while it runs. This covers `.pkg` files, game folders in `homebrew` on the drive, and installed games moved there from the console's storage settings. **Save to** lists those drives too.

### Changed

- **Send diagnostic logs** is on by default for new installs, so problems can be found and fixed sooner. Turn it off with Cross on the About screen; the app remembers that. Updating from 01.023.000 or 01.024.000 keeps the setting you had. The log holds package paths, game titles and title IDs, sizes, speeds, free space and errors, tied only to a random install ID, and is deleted after 30 days.
